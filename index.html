Sections 4–10

Procurement Domains

1

Compute Infrastructure

Section 4 — Servers & Virtualisation
⚠ CRITICAL RISK

SDC-1 servers from 2005–2016 are at or past end-of-life. Hardware failure rates rise exponentially after Year 5. OEM support cessation means no patches, firmware, or spares.

  • Rack-mounted x86-64 blade/rack servers — dual-socket Intel Xeon Scalable / AMD EPYC, PCIe Gen 5
  • High-memory servers (1 TB+ RAM) for DB and in-memory analytics
  • GPU-accelerated nodes for K-AI / AICTE-K inference workloads
  • VMware licensing evaluation — renew or migrate to KVM/Proxmox
  • E-waste decommissioning per E-Waste Management Rules 2022
2

Storage Infrastructure

Section 5 — Primary, Backup & Fabric
HIGH PRIORITY

SDC operates SAN and NAS; total raw storage likely multi-petabyte. DPDP Act 2023 mandates explicit encryption-at-rest, audit logging, and erasure-on-request at the storage layer.

  • All-Flash Arrays (AFA/NVMe) for Tier-1 — SPARK, e-Procurement, land records
  • Hybrid Flash for Tier-2 — file shares, email archiving
  • Scale-out NAS / object storage + air-gapped backup (ransomware protection)
  • LTO-9 tape library for long-term retention and off-site vault
  • FC SAN switch refresh to 32 Gbps Fibre Channel
3

Network Infrastructure

Section 6 — Core, Internet, WAN, LB
HIGH PRIORITY

Both facilities need capacity augmentation to support projected growth in e-Governance apps, K-FON aggregation, and AI-inference workloads. GoI mandates IPv6 readiness across all government infrastructure.

  • 100 GbE spine, 25 GbE downlinks — SDN-capable for multi-tenant VLAN management
  • Dual ISP with BGP failover; 10 Gbps per site, scalable to 40 Gbps
  • KSWAN integration — MPLS/SD-WAN for secure SDC-1 ↔ SDC-2 connectivity
  • ADC/Load Balancer renewal — L7, SSL offload, WAF, GSLB active-active
  • All equipment to support dual-stack IPv4/IPv6 as minimum specification
4

Cybersecurity Infrastructure

Section 7 — Priority Programme
⚠ CRITICAL — CAG FLAGGED

CAG Audit (2017) documented CERT-K vulnerabilities at SDC-1 (Dec 2013) that were never remediated by independent audit. Over 13 years without third-party security audit. Must be treated as a priority programme.

  • NGFW with DPI, SSL/TLS inspection, IPS at 40 Gbps+ — both sites
  • WAF for all public-facing GoK portals; DDoS mitigation with ISP-level scrubbing
  • EDR/XDR for all SDC-managed servers; PAM with session recording & vaulting
  • SIEM with threat intel + UEBA; SOAR for automated response playbooks
  • 24×7 SOC (in-house or managed); STQC/CERT-In empanelled security audit
5

Power, Cooling & Physical

Section 8 — UPS, CRAC, DG, Physical
⚠ CRITICAL — SDC-1 UPS BATTERIES

SDC-1 (2005) requires systematic UPS battery, cooling, and generator assessment. UPS batteries typically replaced every 3–5 years. SDC-2 benefits from Technopark shared utility but SDC-specific systems must be independently maintained.

  • UPS battery string replacement at SDC-1 — minimum 30 min backup at full load
  • Modular UPS in Tier III-compatible 2N or N+1 configuration
  • Precision AC replacement with hot/cold aisle containment; target PUE < 1.6
  • In-row cooling for GPU and high-density storage zones
  • Solar PV installation (Phase 1) — consistent with Kerala solar mandate
6

Backup & Disaster Recovery

Section 9 — DR Site, Backup, Testing
⚠ CRITICAL — DR STATUS UNCLEAR

Warm-standby DR arrangement with Sify Technologies (Dec 2015). Current status — extended, renegotiated, or lapsed — not publicly documented. No published RTO/RPO metrics despite SPARK, e-Procurement, and e-Health dependency.

  • DR site: STQC-empanelled managed provider OR KSITM-owned (Kochi InfoPark / Cyberpark Calicut)
  • Contractual SLA — RTO ≤ 4 hours, RPO ≤ 1 hour for Tier-1 services
  • Enterprise backup software with deduplication, encryption, cloud-tiering
  • Air-gapped backup; 3-2-1 policy enforcement with daily automated validation
  • Bi-annual full DR failover testing with documented RTO/RPO measurement

Section 14

Recommendations

Immediate Actions — April to June 2026
  • ▸Full asset audit of both SDC facilities to establish accurate CMDB, identify EOL equipment, and validate capacity before finalising procurement quantities
  • ▸Commission independent security audit of SDC-1 and SDC-2 by CERT-In empanelled auditor as emergency measure — given absence of third-party audit since 2013
  • ▸Renew or renegotiate the Disaster Recovery arrangement (currently with Sify Technologies) with formally documented RTO/RPO commitments before 30 June 2026
  • ▸Replace UPS battery strings at SDC-1 to eliminate the highest-probability single point of failure for power continuity
Strategic Decisions Required — Q1 to Q2
  • ▸Decide on virtualisation platform path: renew VMware licensing under Broadcom's model, or migrate to open-source hypervisor with commercial support — must resolve before compute hardware procurement
  • ▸Define DR site strategy: managed service (lower capex, higher opex) vs. KSITM-owned facility at Kochi InfoPark or Cyberpark Calicut (higher capex, greater control)
  • ▸Evaluate hybrid cloud model: engage MeitY and NIC to clarify MeghRaj (NIC Cloud) capacity available to GoK agencies to offset on-premises compute demand
Longer-Term Roadmap
  • ▸Target Tier III certification for SDC-2 (Technopark) within 3 years, leveraging the facility's newer infrastructure as foundation
  • ▸Develop a 5-year SDC Capacity Master Plan incorporating projected growth from K-AI, Smart City data platforms, ABDM, and DPDP Act compliance workloads
  • ▸Establish dedicated KSITM-operated SOC co-located with the SDC, with formal MoU with CERT-K and Kerala Police Cyber Cell for threat intelligence sharing
